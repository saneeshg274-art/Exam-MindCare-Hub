# Privacy Policy for Exam&MindCare Hub

**Last Updated:** October 23, 2026

Welcome to Exam&MindCare Hub ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled safely and responsibly.

### 1. Information We Collect & Data Safety
Exam&MindCare Hub is designed to operate primarily offline and locally on your device to assist with your competitive examination preparation (Kerala PSC, UPSC, SSC) and stress relief:
- Profile & Preferences: Display name, target exam category (e.g., Kerala PSC 10th/Degree Level, UPSC Prelims), education level, and language preferences are stored strictly locally on your device.
- Study & Mindfulness Data: Daily study streaks, mock test OMR responses, scores, and meditation timers are saved in local device storage.
- No Sensitive Data: We do not collect or request passwords, financial details, government ID numbers, or biometric data.

### 2. Device Permissions Used
- Notifications (Optional): Used solely to send optional daily study streak reminders and mindfulness prompts. Can be disabled anytime in system settings.
- Storage / PDF Export: Used solely to save your generated OMR test scorecards and revision notes as PDF files to your device storage.

### 3. Data Storage & No Third-Party Sharing
- Local Storage: Your data remains on your device.
- No Sale of Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or study metrics to any advertisers or third parties.

### 4. User Rights & Data Deletion
- You can clear your exam records, test history, or reset study streaks anytime from the in-app Profile screen.
- Uninstalling the application completely deletes all locally stored data.
